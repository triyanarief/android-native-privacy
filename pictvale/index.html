Pictvale: Nonogram

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Pictvale: Nonogram is an offline Android puzzle app. This policy describes the current app's handling of puzzle data. You can play without an account.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your selected puzzle, cell marks, undo and redo history, completed puzzles, introduction status, and haptic, contrast, and cell-size preferences in app-private local storage. It uses these records to resume play and remember your settings. The app has no account service, app-managed server, or cloud sync, and it does not send this saved puzzle data to the developer.

Exporting a copy

Only when you choose Export progress in Settings does Android's document picker ask where to save a JSON copy. The file contains saved puzzle progress and history, completion status, and preferences. It is plain text and is not encrypted by the app. A document or cloud-storage provider you select may store, process, or sync that copy under its own terms. The app does not upload it automatically. This version has no import or restore-from-export feature.

Retention and deletion

Local puzzle data remains until you successfully confirm Reset local data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android, or uninstall. Reset removes the app's saved progress, history, completed puzzles, and preferences; it does not delete exported files outside the app. Delete those copies through the destination where you saved them. There is no account to delete or app-managed cloud recovery. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app, but on some Android devices a system device-to-device transfer may still copy app data.

Permissions and other software

Core play needs no runtime permission. Export uses Android's document picker for the destination you choose. The current app requests no Internet, camera, photo-library, contacts, location, or broad storage permission. It has no photo-import or photo-upload feature and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. AndroidX libraries support the interface and lifecycle. Android, Google Play, and a document provider you choose may handle their own device, installation, diagnostic, or file information under their respective policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. If you email us, you choose what information to include; please do not attach a puzzle export unless you want to share its contents for support.
