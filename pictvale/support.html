Support

Last updated: 30 September 2026

For help with Pictvale: Nonogram, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, and what happened. Please do not send a puzzle export or other private information unless needed for your request.

Using the app

Choose a puzzle pack, fill squares from the row and column clues, and mark squares you know are empty. You can undo, redo, use a hint, and continue saved progress. The app works offline without an account. Settings include haptic feedback, high contrast, and cell-size options.

Exporting progress

Use Settings → Export progress to choose a destination through Android's document picker. The resulting JSON file is a readable copy of saved puzzle progress, history, completions, and preferences. It is not encrypted by the app. This version cannot import that file or restore progress from it, and it does not provide automatic cloud recovery. A cloud-backed destination may sync the copy under that provider's settings.

Resetting local data

Settings → Reset local data asks for confirmation before clearing the app's local progress and preferences. It does not remove files you exported to another location. To delete an export, use the file or cloud-storage provider where you saved it.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local records, export, permissions, backup limits, and deletion.
