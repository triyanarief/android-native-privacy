Support

For help with Shelvenza, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, and what happened. Avoid sending private inventory details unless needed for support.

Using the app

Add items with quantities and locations, track low stock and upcoming dates, and keep a separate shopping list. Shelvenza works without an account or internet connection.

Exporting a copy

Use Export in Settings to save an inventory CSV through Android's document picker. The CSV is a readable copy of inventory items. It excludes the shopping list, quantity-change history, and preferences. This version cannot import the CSV or restore app data from it, and it does not offer automatic cloud recovery.

Privacy
