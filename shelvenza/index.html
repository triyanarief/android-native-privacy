Shelvenza: Home Inventory

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Shelvenza: Home Inventory is an offline Android inventory app developed by Triyan Arif Wibowo. You can use it without an account.

Information on your device

The app stores item names, quantities, units, locations, optional dates and low-stock points, quantity-change history, shopping-list entries, and app preferences in its private local database. It uses these records to show your inventory, low-stock and upcoming-date views, and shopping list. This version has no app server and does not send your inventory to the developer.

CSV export

When you choose Export, Android's document picker lets you select where to save an inventory CSV. It contains item names, quantities, units, locations, dates and low-stock points. It does not include the shopping list or quantity-change history. Your chosen destination may be a cloud-backed provider, whose privacy and backup settings then apply to that file. The app does not export automatically.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete an item, confirm Delete all local data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android, or uninstall. Delete all local data removes inventory, quantity history, shopping entries, and preferences from the app. CSV files saved outside the app must be deleted from their destination separately. The app disables Android automatic backup and device transfer of its private data and does not offer account sync or recovery after uninstall.

Permissions and third parties

The app does not request Internet, contacts, location, camera, or broad storage permission. Android's document picker handles the export destination you choose. The current app has no advertising or analytics SDK; AndroidX libraries support its interface and lifecycle. Google Play, Android, and a document provider you select may process their own installation, diagnostic, or file information under their respective policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.
