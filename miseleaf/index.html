Miseleaf: Recipe Book

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Miseleaf: Recipe Book is an offline Android app for keeping personal recipes. You can use it without creating an account. This policy describes the data handled by the app and the choices available to you.

Information kept on your device

The app stores recipe titles, descriptions, servings, preparation and cooking times, ingredients, steps, categories, notes, favorite choices, photo descriptions, and recipe record details in an app-private local database. It also stores cooking-session progress and your appearance choice locally. These records support the recipe library, search, favorites, cooking mode, and settings. This version has no developer-operated account or recipe server and does not send your recipe content to the developer.

Photos you select

If you choose a recipe photo, the Android Photo Picker gives the app access to that selected item. The app copies it into app-private storage so it can display the recipe photo later. The app does not request camera or broad photo-library permission. You can replace or remove a photo through recipe editing. Photo files and device-specific photo paths are not included in JSON exports.

JSON export and import

Export and import happen only when you choose them. Android's document picker lets you select where to save an export or which file to read for import. An exported JSON file contains recipe text and fields, including ingredients, steps, categories, notes, favorite choices, photo descriptions, recipe identifiers, and record timestamps. It does not contain copied photo files or cooking-session progress. You can review an import preview before committing changes. Matching recipe identifiers can update existing records; an existing device-local photo is retained for a matching record.

The app does not encrypt an exported JSON file. Anyone with access to that file or its storage location can read its contents. A location you select may be managed by a cloud-backed document provider; that provider's storage and privacy practices then apply to the file. The app does not automatically upload exports or provide cloud sync.

Retention, deletion, and backup limits

Recipes and cooking progress remain on the device until you edit or delete them, confirm Delete all local data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android, or uninstall the app. Delete all clears the recipe database and attempts to remove app-managed photo copies. Your appearance choice remains until you change it, clear app storage, or uninstall. Android automatic backup and device transfer are disabled for this app, so the app does not offer automatic recovery after uninstall.

Deleting app data does not remove JSON files you previously exported outside the app. Delete those files from the location you chose, including any cloud-backed provider, separately.

Permissions, libraries, and other services

The current app does not request Internet, camera, contacts, location, or broad media permission. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. AndroidX and Kotlin libraries support the interface and app lifecycle, while Android system components handle selected photos and document access. Google Play, Android, and a document provider you choose may process their own installation, diagnostic, or file information under their respective policies; Miseleaf does not control those services.
