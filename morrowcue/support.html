MorrowCue: Flashcards

Support

For help with MorrowCue, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, what you tried, and what happened. Avoid sending private card content unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Using the app

Create a deck, add cards with front and back text, and review cards when they are due. MorrowCue works without an account or internet connection. This version does not send review reminders.

CSV files

Data tools lets you import or export cards through Android's document picker. Exports are plain-text CSV files containing card text and due dates, so choose a destination you trust. Keep your own exported copy before changing devices or uninstalling.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local study records, CSV files, and deletion.
