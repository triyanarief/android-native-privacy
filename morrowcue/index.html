MorrowCue: Flashcards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

MorrowCue: Flashcards is an offline Android study app developed by Triyan Arif Wibowo. You can use it without an account.

Information on your device

The app stores deck names and descriptions, card fronts and backs, review grades, review times, and due schedules in its private database on your device. This lets you create cards and study without an internet connection. The app does not request Android's internet permission and does not send your study content to a developer server. Card text may contain information you consider private; choose what you enter.

CSV import and export

You can choose a CSV file through Android's document picker to import decks and cards. The app validates the file before saving importable rows. When you export, Android lets you choose where to save a plain-text UTF-8 CSV containing card fronts, backs, deck names, and due dates. The export does not contain the full review-history log. A destination you choose may be cloud-backed and is governed by that provider's settings. The app does not import or export automatically.

Deletion and retention

Cards and decks remain in the app until you delete them or clear the app's storage. Deleting a deck also removes its cards and related review records. You can clear the app's local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Files you exported are outside the app and must be deleted from their chosen destination separately. The app offers no account recovery or automatic cloud sync.

Backup and third parties

The app requests that Android backup of its private app data be disabled. A CSV saved to a provider you choose is a separate, user-controlled copy. The app has no advertising, analytics, or external data-service SDK in its current build. Google Play and Android may separately process installation and diagnostic information under Google's policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.
