Pacefern: Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Pacefern: Habit Tracker is an offline Android habit tracker developed by Triyan Arif Wibowo. You can use it without an account.

Information on your device

The app stores habit names, goals, schedules, reminder times, daily values, and app preferences in private storage on your device. It uses this information to show your progress and, if you enable reminders, to schedule local alerts. The app does not request Android's internet permission and does not send your habit data to a developer server.

Reminders

Reminders are optional. On Android 13 and later, the app may ask for notification permission. A reminder can show a habit name; whether that appears on the lock screen depends on your Android settings. You can disable reminders in the app or change notification and lock-screen settings in Android. The app can reschedule enabled reminders after a device restart.

Export and deletion

When you choose Export, Android's document picker lets you save a CSV file containing your habit records to a destination you select. That destination may be a local folder or a cloud-backed provider you use. Its own privacy and backup settings apply to the exported file. Pacefern does not export data automatically.

The confirmed deletion action in the app removes stored habits and entries and stops reminders for those habits. App preferences remain unless you clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. An exported CSV is outside the app and must be removed from its chosen destination separately.

Backup and third parties

The app disables Android backup and device transfer of its private database and preferences. It does not offer account sync, advertising, or analytics. Its AndroidX interface libraries are packaged with the app; the current app does not include a third-party SDK that receives your habit records. Google Play and Android may separately process installation and diagnostic information under Google's policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.
