Pacefern: Habit Tracker

Support

For help with Pacefern, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, what you tried, and what happened. Please avoid sending private habit details unless they are necessary to explain the issue.

Using the app

Pacefern works without an account. Create a yes/no or quantity habit, choose a schedule or weekly goal, and log progress on your device. Optional reminders depend on your Android notification settings.

Export and recovery

Use Export in the app to save a CSV through Android's document picker. Keep a copy in a location you trust before changing devices or uninstalling. The app does not provide automatic cloud backup or import.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local records, reminders, export, and deletion.
