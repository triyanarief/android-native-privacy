Foliolet: Notes

Support

For help with Foliolet, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send private note text unless it is needed for support.

App lock

App lock uses your configured Android device credential. It controls access to the screen; it does not encrypt saved notes or exported files.

Export and recovery

Use Export in the Privacy tab to save a JSON backup through Android's document picker. The backup contains plain-text notes, so choose a destination you trust. Keep your own copy before changing devices or uninstalling; automatic cloud sync is not provided.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local notes, app lock, backups, and deletion.
