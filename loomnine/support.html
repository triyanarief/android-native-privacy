Loomnine: Sudoku

Support

Last updated: 30 September 2026

For help with Loomnine: Sudoku, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Please do not send your puzzle backup unless it is needed to investigate the issue.

Playing

Choose an Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert puzzle. You can enter numbers or pencil notes, undo and redo moves, check for conflicts, and ask for a logical hint. Progress is saved on this device. The app works without an account or internet connection and does not use photos.

Exporting and restoring

In Settings, choose Export puzzle to save a JSON backup through Android's document picker. The file can contain your puzzle, notes, move history, elapsed time, hints used, and settings. Choose Import puzzle to select a valid backup; review the preview before replacing your current puzzle and settings. A backup from the immediately previous version with a complete puzzle can be imported, but an older empty backup without an app identifier may be rejected for safety. A file saved to a cloud-backed destination is handled by that provider.

Unreadable saves and deletion

If the app reports an unreadable save, you can export the unreadable data before starting fresh. Starting fresh preserves a separate recovery copy in the app's private files. Delete local data resets the current puzzle and settings but does not remove backups you exported or a preserved recovery copy. To remove app-private recovery copies, clear the app's storage in Android or uninstall; delete exported backups from their chosen destination separately.
