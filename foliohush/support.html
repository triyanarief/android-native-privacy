Support

Last updated: 30 September 2026

For help with Foliohush: Reading Log, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Please do not attach a backup or copy private notes into your message unless they are needed to investigate and you choose to share them.

Using the app

Add a book manually, place it in To Read, Reading, or Finished, update its current page, and keep one private note with it. Search and filter your library or view simple status counts in Overview. This version works without an account or external book catalog. It does not read ebooks, fetch book covers, or accept book photos.

Backing up your books

In Settings, choose Export data and select a destination in Android's document picker. The JSON backup is readable, unencrypted text and includes your book records, progress, and private notes. Keep it somewhere you control. A cloud-backed provider you select may sync or share it. Export does not delete the books on your device.

Restoring from a backup

In Settings, choose Import data and select a compatible Foliohush JSON backup. Review the proposed additions and updates before confirming. Import can merge records; it does not simply replace the whole library. An invalid or unsupported file is not imported. If you encounter a problem, keep the original file and contact support without sending it unless needed.

Deleting local data

You can delete an individual book or use Settings → Delete all local data to remove books, progress, and notes from the app. This does not delete exported files or reset appearance preferences. Delete exported files separately from their destination. Android app backup is disabled in this version, so do not assume automatic recovery after uninstalling or clearing app storage.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about locally stored records, drafts, JSON files, document providers, and deletion.
