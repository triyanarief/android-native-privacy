Packsprig: Packing Lists

Support

For help with Packsprig, email hello.triyan@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid sending private trip details unless they are needed for support.

Using your lists

Create trips and checklist items, or start from a built-in template. Your lists remain on the device. Packsprig does not provide an account or automatic cloud backup.

Sharing

Copy text uses Android's clipboard; Share uses Android's Sharesheet and an app you choose. Once copied or shared, that text is handled by the destination. Review it before sharing.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local lists, sharing, backup, and deletion.
