Packsprig: Packing Lists

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Packsprig: Packing Lists is an offline Android packing-list app developed by Triyan Arif Wibowo. You can use it without an account.

Information on your device

The app stores trip names, optional dates, checklist items, quantities, categories, notes, packed status, personal templates, and introduction and display preferences in private app storage. Built-in templates are packaged with the app. Your information powers trip lists, packing progress, the archive, and templates. This version has no app server and does not send your lists to the developer.

Sharing is your choice

If you choose Copy text, a plain-text checklist is placed on Android's clipboard. If you choose Share, Android offers the same text to an app you select. Android, the clipboard, and that receiving app may handle or retain the copy under their own rules. Packsprig cannot remove a copy after it leaves the app. The app does not share lists automatically or provide file export in this version.

Retention and deletion

Trips remain on your device until you delete local data or uninstall the app. You can edit or remove individual checklist items and personal templates. The confirmed Delete all local data action removes trips, checklist items, and personal templates. Built-in templates and app preferences remain. Text already copied or shared outside the app must be removed from its destination separately.

Backup, permissions, and third parties

The app disables automatic Android backup and excludes its private database and preferences from Android cloud backup and device transfer. It offers no account sync or cross-device restore. The app does not request Internet, location, contacts, camera, storage, or notification access. It uses AndroidX and Jetpack libraries for its interface and lifecycle, without an advertising or analytics SDK in this version. Google Play and Android may separately process installation and diagnostic information under Google's policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.
